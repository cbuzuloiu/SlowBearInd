Residential Electrical

Complete electrical solutions for homes of all ages and sizes — from routine upgrades to full rewires. Safe, clean, and professional work that causes minimal disruption to your daily life.

Your home's electrical system is one of the most critical — and often overlooked — elements of the property. Whether you're moving into an older home, renovating, adding new appliances, or simply experiencing problems with your current setup, SlowBear Industries has the expertise to handle it all.

We work across all types of residential properties: period homes with older wiring, modern builds, extensions, and new constructions. Every job starts with a thorough assessment of your current system so we can give you honest, accurate advice about what's needed — nothing more, nothing less.

All our residential work is completed to current safety standards and is fully certified. We handle the paperwork, inspections, and compliance certificates so you don't have to worry about a thing.

How It Works

Our Process

From your first call to the final sign-off — here's exactly what to expect when you work with us.

1

Free Site Assessment

We visit your property, assess the current electrical setup, listen to what you need, and identify any issues. No charge, no obligation.

2

Detailed Quote

You receive a clear, itemised written quote covering all labour, materials, and any certification fees. No hidden costs.

3

Schedule at Your Convenience

We work around your schedule. For larger jobs we'll discuss planned outage windows so you can prepare in advance.

4

Professional Work

Our team arrives on time, works cleanly and respectfully in your home, and keeps you updated throughout the job.

5

Testing & Inspection

Every circuit and connection is tested before we leave. We don't sign off on work that hasn't been verified to be safe.

6

Compliance Certificate

You receive a full electrical completion certificate, which you'll need for insurance, property sales, and regulatory compliance.
