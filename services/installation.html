Electrical Installation

Whether you're adding a single fixture or fitting out a whole building from scratch, we install it correctly, safely, and to a standard that lasts.

Installation is where most of our work begins. Getting a new electrical system, piece of equipment, or fixture installed correctly from the start saves significant cost and hassle further down the line — every installation we do is treated with the same level of care regardless of size.

We handle installation projects of all scales: a single EV charger, a full outdoor lighting scheme, a complete building fit-out, or a standby generator system. In every case, we survey first, plan the installation properly, and execute it cleanly with full testing and documentation on completion.

We can also install equipment that you supply yourself. If you've purchased a specific fixture, appliance, or system and need it professionally wired and commissioned, we're happy to take that on alongside our own supply-and-install work.

How It Works

Our Process

From scoping the job to handing over the keys — here's how a typical installation project runs.

1

Requirements Consultation

We discuss exactly what you need installed, where, and how it will be used. We ask the right questions upfront to avoid changes later.

2

Site Survey

We visit the site to assess the existing electrical infrastructure, identify any constraints, and confirm the most practical installation approach.

3

Installation Quote

You receive a fixed-price quote covering all supply, labour, and any incidental materials. No open-ended estimates.

4

Supply & Install

We source quality materials, arrive on the agreed date, and complete the installation cleanly and efficiently. All cable runs are neatly managed and concealed wherever possible.

5

Testing & Commissioning

Every installation is fully tested before we leave. For equipment like generators and EV chargers, we run full commissioning procedures and confirm correct operation.

6

Handover & Documentation

We walk you through what was installed, provide all certificates and warranty documentation, and answer any questions before we go.
