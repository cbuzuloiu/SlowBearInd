Electrical Remediation

Electrical problems don't fix themselves. We diagnose accurately, resolve completely, and leave your installation safe, compliant, and certified — no corners cut.

Remediation is about solving problems — and solving them properly. Whether you've received an unsatisfactory inspection report, noticed warning signs in your property, or inherited an electrical system of unknown age and condition, we'll carry out a thorough diagnosis and give you a clear picture of what needs to be done and why.

We don't believe in alarming clients into unnecessary work. Our assessments are honest: we'll tell you what genuinely needs addressing immediately, what can wait, and what is simply an advisory recommendation. That approach has built us a reputation as electricians people trust.

Once the scope is agreed, we execute the remediation systematically, testing as we go. Every job ends with a new compliance certificate confirming the installation is safe — giving you the documentation you need for insurance, property transactions, and regulatory compliance.

How It Works

Our Process

From fault identification to compliance sign-off — a clear, structured approach to resolving electrical problems.

1

Diagnosis & Fault Finding

We carry out a methodical electrical investigation to identify the root cause of the problem — not just the visible symptom. This is where getting things right starts.

2

Condition Assessment

We assess the broader state of the installation around the fault — checking whether related systems are also affected or at risk. Problems rarely exist in isolation.

3

Remediation Plan

We present a clear written plan covering exactly what needs to be done, in what order, and at what cost. We separate urgent safety issues from advisory improvements.

4

Phased Works Execution

Remediation is carried out systematically, with testing at each stage. For larger jobs, we phase the work to minimise disruption and keep the property safe throughout.

5

Testing & Verification

Every remediated circuit and connection is retested to confirm it meets the required standards. We don't move on until the test results say it's right.

6

Compliance Sign-Off

On completion, you receive an updated satisfactory electrical inspection certificate — the formal documentation that the installation is now safe and compliant.
